# Original free template: Cement plant technical RFQ & spares inquiry (v1)

Publisher: **Infinity for Cement Organization** — https://www.cementequipment.org/cement-plant-rfq-sourcing/

**Download:** https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mohamedomar1987/cement-ai-company-assets/master/free-tools/cement-plant-rfq-intake-template.csv

This 20-line blank CSV is a no-macro, editable checklist for plant engineers and procurement teams. It has no supplier contact list, no manufacturer manuals and no proprietary plant data. Open in a spreadsheet editor. Each line represents one requested item and its identifying specification.

### Workflow
1. Start with the machine manufacturer and model. Enter the exact part number, drawing revision and dimensions **only if confirmed**.
2. State duty, material, quantity, certificates, destination and required date. For interchangeable items, state what evidence an alternative must satisfy; do **not** assume that visually similar parts are compatible.
3. Send the populated file, together with available non-confidential photographs and approved drawings, to the address displayed on the [RFQ service page](https://www.cementequipment.org/cement-plant-rfq-sourcing/). The page provides a free initial requirement screening; any paid follow-up scope or fees must be agreed separately.
4. Verify supplier identity, quality certifications, logistics, import regulations, contract terms and fit-for-purpose independently before purchasing or installing parts.

### Confidentiality and engineering checks
Do not send plant trade secrets, employee/customer personal data, contractual prices or export-controlled drawings before the recipient and a suitable confidentiality basis are verified.
