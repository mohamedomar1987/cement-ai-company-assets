# Free Cement CBAM 2026 Data-Evidence Intake Register (non-compliance tool)

Publisher: Infinity for Cement Organization. Original 25-line blank CSV register, offered as an informational data-collection checklist. This does **not** calculate embedded emissions, determine CN classification, replace monitoring methodology, produce reports, or certify legal CBAM compliance. Keep production/energy values and confidential evidence in internal company systems, NOT on public GitHub or an open webform.

**Official primary sources:**
- European Commission: https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism/cbam-legislation-and-guidance_en
- Commission publication of 14 August 2026: https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/news/european-commission-publishes-series-guidance-documents-support-cbam-implementation-definitive-2026-08-14_en
- See **Guidance 5a (cement)**, Guidance 3 (embedded-emissions methodologies) and current legislation. The CBAM definitive period began 1 January 2026. Check revisions and specific product applicability.

**Use:** Create one row per evidence item—examples include installation boundaries, clinker/cement output, material and fuel measurement records, process emissions, lab analysis, meter calibration and verifier questions. Confirm the responsible data owner and physical measurement basis. Document gaps and require qualified review before generating any statutory figures.

Do not upload this completed sheet publicly or share sensitive data with any third party without an appropriate legal/contractual basis. This free original register is NOT a substitute for a verified CBAM calculation or specialist legal/environmental advice.
